# Business Insights

## Executive Summary

The analysis covers approximately 1.1 million synthetic FMCG transactions across 2021–2023.

The business generated approximately ₹47.29 Cr in net sales and ₹18.22 Cr in gross profit, resulting in an overall gross margin of approximately 38.5%.

## 1. Promotion Margin Impact

Promotional transactions show materially lower profitability than non-promotional transactions.

The dashboard shows approximately:

- Non-promotional margin: 40.0%
- Promotional margin: 21.4%

This highlights the importance of evaluating promotions based on profitability and incremental value rather than revenue growth alone.

## 2. Category Performance

Snacks are the strongest category by gross profit contribution.

This suggests that high-performing categories should receive focused attention when evaluating assortment, inventory allocation and commercial strategy.

## 3. Channel Performance

Hypermarket is the dominant sales channel by net sales.

This makes the channel particularly important for revenue planning, commercial strategy and inventory availability.

## 4. Country Performance

Italy and Spain are the leading markets by revenue.

The Netherlands contributes the lowest revenue among the analyzed countries, highlighting differences in market scale across the portfolio.

## 5. SKU Performance

The SKU analysis shows that a relatively small group of products contributes significantly to total revenue.

Monitoring high-performing SKUs can support assortment decisions, inventory prioritization and commercial planning.

## 6. Supply Chain

The overall stockout rate is approximately 3.0%.

Although relatively small compared with total transactions, stockouts represent a potential source of lost sales and should be monitored alongside demand and inventory planning.

## Recommendations

1. Evaluate promotional campaigns using profit impact rather than revenue uplift alone.
2. Prioritize high-performing categories and SKUs in assortment and inventory planning.
3. Monitor hypermarket performance closely due to its dominant revenue contribution.
4. Investigate lower-performing markets for potential growth opportunities.
5. Track stockout patterns to reduce potential revenue leakage.

## Note

The dataset used for this project is synthetic and intended for portfolio and analytical demonstration purposes.
